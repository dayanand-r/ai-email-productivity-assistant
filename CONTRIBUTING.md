
# 🤝 Contributing

Thank you for your interest in this project! This repository is a **portfolio case study** documenting the concept, design, and impact of an AI-powered communication assistant. It does not contain source code, but contributions in the form of ideas, feedback, and discussions are welcome.

---

## How You Can Contribute

### 💬 Share Feedback
- Open an [Issue](../../issues) to share your thoughts, suggestions, or questions about the project approach
- Let me know if something in the documentation could be explained more clearly

### 💡 Suggest Improvements
- Have ideas for enhancing the solution design or methodology? Open an Issue with the label `enhancement`
- Suggestions for additional sections or documentation are welcome

### 🐛 Report Issues
- Found a typo, broken link, or formatting issue? Open an Issue with the label `bug`
- Please describe what you found and where it's located

### 🗣️ Start a Discussion
- Use the [Discussions](../../discussions) tab (if enabled) to share related experiences or ask questions
- Topics like prompt engineering approaches, no-code AI solutions, or knowledge-grounded generation are all welcome

---

## Contribution Guidelines

### Before Contributing
- Read the [README](README.md) to understand the project context and scope
- Check existing [Issues](../../issues) to avoid duplicates

### When Opening an Issue
- Use a clear and descriptive title
- Provide as much context as possible
- Be respectful and constructive

### What This Repository Does NOT Accept
- ❌ Pull requests containing code (this is a documentation-only repository)
- ❌ Proprietary or confidential information of any kind
- ❌ Content that identifies specific companies, platforms, or internal systems
- ❌ Customer data or any personally identifiable information

---

## Code of Conduct

Please be respectful, constructive, and inclusive in all interactions. This project follows standard open-source etiquette:

- Be welcoming and friendly
- Be patient and empathetic
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the community

---

## Questions?

If you have any questions about this project, feel free to open an Issue or reach out. I'm happy to discuss the approach, share learnings, or help others exploring similar solutions.

---

*Thank you for being part of this journey! 🚀*
