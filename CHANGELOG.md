
# 📋 Changelog

All notable changes and iterations of the AI-Powered Communication Assistant are documented here.

This project follows a narrative changelog format to document the evolution of the solution from initial concept to production-ready deployment.

---

## [V4.0] — Production Release ✅

**Status:** Deployed & Validated

### What Changed
- Fine-tuned model parameters and prompt structure for optimal accuracy
- Achieved consistent empathetic, complete, and accurate output
- Knowledge Repository matching refined to reliably surface the right general information for each customer scenario

### Results
- ✅ Production-ready quality — drafts consistently pass Quality Review on first submission
- ✅ Empathy, completeness, and accuracy all at target levels
- ✅ Reliable and consistent performance across different customer situations

---

## [V3.0] — Refinement

**Status:** Improved but required tone adjustments

### What Changed
- Improved Knowledge Repository matching — agent more accurately identifies relevant general information
- Better extraction of key details from customer emails
- Refined prompt structure for completeness

### Known Issues
- Tone was sometimes inconsistent — occasionally too formal or too casual for the situation
- Required further parameter tuning to balance accuracy with natural tone

---

## [V2.0] — Knowledge Integration

**Status:** Improved empathy but inconsistent information surfacing

### What Changed
- Improved knowledge base integration for better information retrieval
- Enhanced prompts for empathetic communication
- Added more detailed instructions for situation-based information matching

### Known Issues
- Inconsistent at matching Knowledge Repository information to customer situations
- Sometimes surfaced information that wasn't directly relevant to the specific scenario
- Needed better configuration for contextual accuracy

---

## [V1.0] — Initial Prototype

**Status:** Basic functionality with significant gaps

### What Changed
- Initial agent design and configuration on the enterprise AI platform
- Basic prompt engineering for email analysis and response drafting
- Connected Knowledge Repository as a knowledge source
- Configured document reading and conversation memory

### Known Issues
- Missed empathy cues — drafts felt robotic and impersonal
- Poor Knowledge Repository matching — didn't surface the right general information
- Outputs required significant manual editing before being usable
- Multiple glitches in initial configuration

---

## [V0.0] — Before the AI Agent

**Status:** Manual process with frequent quality issues

### How It Worked
- All email drafting done manually without AI assistance
- Relied entirely on personal knowledge and memory for information
- High rejection rate from Quality Review
- Significant time spent on rework and revision cycles

### Pain Points
- ❌ Lack of empathy in responses
- ❌ Missing or incorrect general information for customer situations
- ❌ Inconsistent quality across emails
- ❌ Low productivity and confidence

> 💡 *This is the baseline that inspired the creation of the AI-Powered Communication Assistant.*
