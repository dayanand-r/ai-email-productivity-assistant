## The Story in 60 Seconds

**The Problem:** As a new team member in customer operations, my email drafts were frequently rejected during Quality Review. I lacked empathy in my writing, often included wrong or irrelevant general information, and missed customer concerns. Every rejection meant rework, delays, and declining confidence.

**The Turning Point:** Instead of accepting this as a learning curve, I asked: *"What if I could build something to help me get it right the first time?"*

**The Solution:** Using an enterprise AI platform's no-code agent builder, I designed a custom AI communication assistant — entirely through prompt engineering and configuration. The agent analyzes customer emails, identifies the right general product information from a Knowledge Repository for each situation, and generates empathetic, complete draft text.

**The Workflow:** The AI drafts the text content → I copy it to Outlook → manually format → review and edit → submit for Quality Review. The AI assists with drafting; I control everything else.

**The Impact:** Rejection rates dropped dramatically. Drafting speed improved significantly. Email quality became consistent. I went from struggling to exceeding performance targets.

**The Skills:** Prompt engineering, AI agent design, knowledge base integration, iterative development, business problem-solving, responsible AI practices — all without writing a single line of code.

## Common Questions

**Q: Did you write any code?**
No. The entire solution was built using no-code AI platform capabilities and prompt engineering.

**Q: Does the AI send emails?**
No. The AI only generates draft text. I manually copy it to Outlook, format it, review it, and submit it for approval.

**Q: What data does the AI access?**
Only general product information from the Knowledge Repository — the same information available to customers. No internal policies, no confidential data.

**Q: How long did it take to build?**
The initial prototype (V1) was built relatively quickly. Iterating from V1 to production-ready V4 took multiple refinement cycles over a period of time.

**Q: Could this be used by other team members?**
Yes — the approach is scalable. While I built it for personal use, the concept could be adopted by others facing similar challenges.
